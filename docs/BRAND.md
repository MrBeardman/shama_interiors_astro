# Brand: Schama Interiors

Taken from the old site's Elementor kit and logo, then warmed and modernised.

| Token | Value | Source / use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#4A4642` | old primary; headings |
| Text | `#5F5A54` | darker than the old `#7A7A7A`, for contrast |
| Terracotta | `#A85C38` | old accent; buttons, numbers, closing band |
| Amber | `#FFA300` | old highlight; dots, progress, accents on dark only (too light for text on paper) |
| Paper / Sand | `#FAF7F2` / `#F0EADF` | new warm backgrounds (old site: white) |
| Deep | `#2A2724` | new dark bands, footer |

Type: **Josefin Sans** (old display face, self-hosted, variable), **Inter** for body (replaces Arial).

Signature shapes: the logo's flowing signature line (the `Swash` flourish that draws itself), the round portrait of Jana,
square-cornered photo frames.

Motion: calm and precise, no bounce. Curves and durations follow the `review-animations` standards
(`--ease-out`, `--ease-io`). Words rise from masks, photos wipe open with an inner settle and parallax, paragraph words
light up on scroll, timeline lines draw, cards stack, the price counts up, the closing band grows edge to edge, project
photos morph between pages through view transitions.
